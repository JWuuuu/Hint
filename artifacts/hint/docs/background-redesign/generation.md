# Homepage background redesign

The original background was extracted from the rendered homepage without its content. `original-background.html` preserves the original gradients, two SVG layers and grain at 440 × 956; `home-original.jpg` is the standalone render.

The new background was generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the extracted background only as a palette reference. It is saved as `../../public/brand/backgrounds/home-celestial-v2.png` (851 × 1848), and consumed by `HomeBackground.tsx`. The image uses the app base URL, fills an absolute decorative layer and is hidden in the dark theme, which retains the existing night palette.

The four homepage sections have exactly the same bounds before and after replacement on iPhone 17 Pro Max; measurements are saved in `layout-verification.json`.

Verified on iPhone 17 Pro (402 × 874) and iPhone 17 Pro Max (440 × 956). Frontend typecheck and production build passed. The production copy of the PNG has the same SHA-256 hash as the source asset.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background image for the Hint tarot iPhone app, a standalone portrait wallpaper with no UI. Input image 1 is ONLY a reference for the app's warm ivory, pale lavender and muted champagne palette. Completely redesign the celestial artwork from scratch; remove all of the tangled intersecting loops and scattered constellation connections in the reference. Create ONE image, portrait aspect ratio approximately 440:956 (around 1080x2340), edge to edge, no frame. Art direction: a calm, sophisticated light celestial atmosphere, near-white ivory #FCFAF6 across the center, extremely soft lilac and champagne atmospheric light washing gently into only the outer corners, clean smooth finish with very fine almost imperceptible paper grain. In the UPPER RIGHT corner only, one elegantly drafted, coherent thin champagne orbital ellipse, cropped naturally off the top and right edge; precise smooth continuous geometry, not hand-drawn scribbles, no multiple crossing orbits. A single small pale lavender moon-like sphere softly integrated along that orbit, subtle and understated, with at most three tiny delicate stars. Keep all recognizable celestial decoration confined to the uppermost 15% and outer right 12% of the artwork, so top-left buttons and all page text remain legible. Leave at least the central 80% of the canvas almost plain warm ivory with very low contrast and no objects or lines. At the bottom edge a barely perceptible mist of pale lilac fading smoothly to ivory, with no recognizable shapes. The result should feel like a thoughtfully art-directed premium mobile app background, quiet and spacious. No text, letters, logos, UI, buttons, cards, phone mockup, border, watermark, dense star field, dark night sky, galaxy photo, connected constellation diagrams, scribbles, rainbow clouds, harsh gradients, muddy beige, repeated pattern or glitter.
